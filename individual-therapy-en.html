What you can bring to therapy

I work with adults who feel worn down by anxiety, panic attacks, intrusive thoughts, self-criticism, guilt, or shame. We can also talk about eating difficulties, burnout, loss of interest in everyday life, and patterns in close relationships.

You might have a clear concern, such as finding it hard to set boundaries. Or you may simply feel that you want things to be different. Either is a starting point; we can work together to put what matters to you into words.

How I approach our work

My individual work draws on cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), dialectical behavior therapy (DBT), and personality-oriented reconstructive psychotherapy. We look at real situations from your life and decide together what would be useful to focus on.

In CBT, we explore how thoughts, emotions, and actions influence one another, test familiar assumptions, and try different responses. DBT skills can be part of working with intense emotions, impulses, and boundaries. Reconstructive psychotherapy gives us a way to explore how past experiences connect with present expectations and recurring relationship patterns.

Our first session

The first meeting is a full-length, paid session. I will listen, ask questions, and help clarify what you want support with. We will talk about your expectations, possible goals, and how future sessions could work.

Please let me know about any diagnosis, prescribed medication, or previous therapy that is relevant to what brings you here. I do not prescribe medication. We can discuss whether speaking to a medical professional would be helpful.

Between sessions

We return to what changes in daily life: whether you notice anxiety sooner, find it easier to express a need, or respond differently in a difficult conversation. At times I may suggest a small exercise or something to notice so we can explore what works for you.

How long we work together depends on your concerns and how the work develops. We can discuss a meeting schedule at the start and review it along the way. Decisions about continuing take your needs and my professional scope into account.
