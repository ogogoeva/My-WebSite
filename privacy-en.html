Personal Data Processing Policy

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit Anastasia Goeva’s website or use the contact details listed on it. The data controller is Anastasia Sergeyevna Goeva. For privacy enquiries, email anagoeva7@gmail.com.

2. What information is used and why

The website has no booking forms or user accounts. If you contact me by email or through a messaging app, I receive the information available through that channel, such as your name or username, contact details and message. I use it to respond, arrange, reschedule or cancel an appointment, or discuss working together. For an initial enquiry, your contact details and a brief note about why you’re getting in touch are enough.

3. Legal grounds for processing

I process the information needed to respond to your request and to arrange or provide services at your request. Where the law requires consent, it is obtained separately. Information may also be processed to meet legal obligations.

4. Retention

Retention depends on why you contacted me, whether we enter into an agreement and any legal requirements. Once the information is no longer needed and there is no other lawful basis to keep it, processing stops and the information is deleted or anonymised as applicable.

5. Hosting and external services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses, when pages load. When you follow a link to a messaging app, YouTube or a bookseller, that service may process information under its own policies.

6. Your rights

You may ask about how your information is handled, request corrections, restriction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Email anagoeva7@gmail.com to make a request. You may also contact the relevant supervisory authority or seek a judicial remedy.
