When to consider coming together

Couples come to me when they are tired of familiar arguments, feel unheard, or notice that life together has started to feel like sharing a home without much closeness. Changes such as moving, having a baby, or coping with a loss may also bring you to therapy.

You can come if you are unsure what comes next: whether you want to rebuild closeness, understand each other better, or make arrangements after a separation. At our first meeting we will talk about what each of you hopes to work on.

How I use EFT

I use emotionally focused therapy (EFT) with couples. We look at the cycle that keeps happening between you: what starts the tension, how each person tries to cope, and when it becomes harder to hear one another.

We explore the feelings and needs behind familiar reactions, especially those that are difficult to express during an argument. My role is to help you both understand the pattern and explore different ways of responding. Both of you need space to be heard.

Why we may meet individually

After one or two joint sessions, I usually suggest one or two separate meetings with each partner before we return to working together. This helps me understand each person’s experience and what feels difficult to say in a joint session.

These meetings are part of the couples work. We discuss in advance how important information can be brought into joint sessions. If you would also like ongoing individual therapy, you will need a different therapist for that work.

The first session and what follows

We get to know one another, discuss what concerns each of you, and begin to agree on goals. You can talk about doubts and about subjects you are not ready to explore yet. Both partners need space to speak.

We will discuss frequency and duration in light of your situation. I cannot set an exact timeline or promise a particular outcome. We will keep reviewing what you notice changing and what remains difficult.

Getting ready to meet online

Choose a private place, check your connection, and put your device on a stable surface. Set aside other tasks so that you can both speak and listen. We will agree on the platform and any practical details before the session.
